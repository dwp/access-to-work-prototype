Registered director information

What you'll need to enter in this application

Your Unique Taxpayer Reference (opens in new tab), if you have one.

Your Companies House (opens in new tab) registration number, if you have one.

What you'll need to provide when a case manager contacts you

We'll ask you to confirm you are receiving an income from the business.

If you've been trading for more than a year, we'll ask for your accounts or submitted tax returns that show an annual turnover of more than the lower earnings limit.

If you've been trading for less than a year, we'll ask for your business plan (opens in new tab) and 3-year financial forecast that show you expect a turnover of more than the lower earnings limit.

The lower earnings limit for 2026 to 2027 is £6,708.

Previous years' lower earnings limits
2025 to 2026 - £6,500
2024 to 2025 - £6,396
